    How this replaces renting an LLM to read your data

    Embedding every row and asking an LLM (RAG) means a per-call bill, your data leaving the building, and an answer you can't re-check. CSV Forge keeps only rules that survive data they never saw, hands you a tiny model you own and run for nothing, and signs a receipt anyone can re-verify. Nothing uploads — this page has no server.
